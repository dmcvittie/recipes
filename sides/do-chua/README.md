# Do Chua (Pickled Carrot and Daikon)

Quick pickle for banh mi and com plates. No boiling required. Edible in an hour, better overnight, keeps a month refrigerated.

## Ingredients

For ~1 lb of vegetables, roughly 2:1 daikon to carrot:

**Massage**

- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tsp sugar

**Brine**

- 1/2 cup sugar
- 3/4 cup rice vinegar
- 1 cup lukewarm water

Brine ratio is 2 sugar : 3 vinegar : 4 water. Scale freely.

## Instructions

1. Peel and julienne the daikon and carrot into matchsticks about 3" long.
2. Toss with the salt and sugar and massage 2-3 minutes, until a piece bends into a U without snapping.
3. Rinse well and squeeze dry. Don't skip this or it will be too salty.
4. Stir the brine until the sugar dissolves. Pack the vegetables into a container and pour the brine over to cover.
5. Refrigerate at least 1 hour, ideally overnight.

## Notes

- Keep everything submerged — anything above the liquid line browns. A piece of parchment on top works as a weight.
- Daikon smells strong while pickling. Keep it sealed.
- 1 lb of vegetables fills a 32 oz deli container.
