# Nuoc Cham

Dipping sauce and dressing for com and bun dishes, and for banh mi.
Improves after a day in the fridge. Keeps 2 weeks refrigerated.

## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp lime juice
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 10 tbsp fish sauce
- 3/4 cup water
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp red pepper flakes

Yields ~2 cups. Comfortable in a 32 oz deli container.

## Instructions

1. Combine everything except the pepper flakes and stir until the sugar is fully dissolved.
2. For a mixed crowd, pour off about half as a mild batch, then stir the pepper flakes into the remainder.
3. Refrigerate. Taste again the next day — add sugar if too sour, water if too sharp, fish sauce if it lacks depth.

## Notes

- Runs brighter than a traditional 5:1 fish sauce to lime ratio. For a more fish-sauce-forward version, drop to 2 tbsp lime.
- 2 cups was right for ~11 adults with a little left over. Halve it for a family dinner.
