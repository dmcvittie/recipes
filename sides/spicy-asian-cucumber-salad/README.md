# Spicy Asian Cucumber Salad

## Ingredients
- 5-6 Persian cucumbers (or 1 large English cucumber)
- 1 tsp of kosher salt
- 1 tsp of sugar
- 3 cloves of garlic, minced
- 2 Tbsp of rice vinegar
- 1 Tbsp of low sodium soy sauce
- 2 tsp of sesame oil
- 1-2 Tbsp of chili crisp or chili oil (more or less depending on how spicy you want it)
- 1 tsp of toasted sesame seeds
- 1 green onion, thinly sliced (for garnish)

## Directions

### Cucumbers
1. Smash the cucumbers with the flat side of a knife or a rolling pin until they crack and split.
2. Cut the smashed cucumbers into bite-sized pieces.
3. Toss the cucumbers with the salt and sugar in a colander and let them drain for 15-20 minutes. This pulls out excess water so the dressing doesn't get diluted.

### Dressing
1. Combine the garlic, rice vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil and chili crisp in a bowl.
2. Stir to combine and set aside.

### Assembly
1. Pat the drained cucumbers dry with a paper towel and transfer to a serving bowl.
2. Pour the dressing over the cucumbers and toss to coat.
3. Top with sesame seeds and green onion. Serve immediately, or chill for 30 minutes for better flavor.
